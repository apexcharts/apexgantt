Localization & RTL

Pick a language to re-render the Gantt. locale.dateLocale localizes the timeline month / weekday names, locale.messages translates the toolbar, context menu, task form, tooltip, and validation, and locale.direction: 'rtl' flows the whole chart right-to-left, including a mirrored time axis (the earliest date sits on the right). 日本語 uses the first-party locale pack instead: locale: { pack: ApexGanttLocales.ja }. Try the toolbar's add / edit, hover a bar, and right-click a row.

Localized here: timeline month / weekday labels (via the dayjs locale object), toolbar + context-menu + task-form strings, bar tooltip labels, validation messages, and bar aria-labels. For Arabic / Hebrew the time axis, task-list panel, and chrome mirror right-to-left. The 日本語 pack (status: draft) also translates the column titles, durations (36日), filter operators and every aria-label, shows dates as 2025/12/29, starts weeks on Monday (12/29～, 1/5～), and zooming out shows fiscal quarters and years from April (2025年度 第4四半期, 2026年度). calendar.weekStart and calendar.fiscalYearStartMonth override the pack's. Defaults (no locale) stay English, left-to-right.
